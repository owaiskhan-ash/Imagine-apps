Case study / WhatsApp scheduling app

Ghost

A Windows and Android WhatsApp scheduling app with recurring schedules, contact management and a persistent queue.

WindowsAndroidScheduling

Project icon illustration. Explore the source for the latest implementation.

A scheduling workspace for WhatsApp

Ghost is a local WhatsApp scheduler for Windows and Android. Its interface brings together contacts, scheduled messages and a queue, with light and dark neumorphic themes. It is designed to help people organise messages that need to be sent at a particular time.

Scheduling and contacts

The project supports one-time and recurring schedules, including daily, weekly, monthly and custom intervals. Contacts can be imported, and messages can use variables such as a recipient’s name or the date. Multi-recipient dispatch, queue history and pause or resume controls are also documented.

Responsible messaging and platform requirements

Ghost relies on WhatsApp and device-specific automation. The Windows documentation states that WhatsApp must be signed in and the computer awake and unlocked at dispatch time. Only message people who have consented, and consider the official WhatsApp Business Platform for sustained high-volume campaigns.

What this project shows

Ghost demonstrates a utility app designed around a repeated workflow: prepare a message, choose recipients, set a schedule and monitor the queue. See the repository for platform-specific installation instructions, current releases and limitations.
