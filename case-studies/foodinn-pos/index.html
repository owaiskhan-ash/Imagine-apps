A point of sale for restaurant orders

Food Inn POS is a restaurant point-of-sale project centred on menu selection and order management. It brings a menu, cart and billing summary together to support a straightforward order-taking workflow.

From menu to kitchen

The interface includes menu search and categories, order types, quantities, discounts and an estimated tax setting. Orders can be recorded and reviewed, with options to print a receipt or kitchen ticket. Payment choices include cash, UPI and card.

What the app records

The project records payment information locally; it does not process a transaction. Tax settings and applicable rates should be verified before billing. The repository is the best place to review the implementation and its current limitations.

What this project shows

Food Inn POS demonstrates how a focused restaurant interface can bring ordering, billing and kitchen handoff into one workspace. It is an example of tailoring a point-of-sale flow to the needs of a specific business.
