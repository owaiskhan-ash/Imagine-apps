A point of sale for a fragrance catalogue

PoppyBlue POS is a retail point-of-sale project built around a fragrance catalogue. The interface brings product selection and checkout together with tools for invoices, customer information and store reporting. The goal is to make common store tasks easier to navigate from a single workspace.

From catalogue to checkout

The project includes a searchable product catalogue, a shopping cart, discounts and payment method selection. Invoices can include customer details and tax information. Its inventory view supports product-level stock and tax settings, while reports summarise sales and provide CSV exports.

Practical considerations

Inventory counts and tax classifications need to be checked before use. The source notes that stock is not synced with Shopify and that operational tax exports are not a substitute for GST filing. Authentication and data services require appropriate configuration.

What this project shows

This project is an example of how a focused business application can combine a familiar checkout flow with the supporting tools a small retail team needs. Open the app to explore its interface. Access is limited to authorised staff.
