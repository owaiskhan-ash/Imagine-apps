Everything you might want to know before sharing your idea.

What can Imagine build?

Imagine helps turn ideas into apps and websites, from business tools and point-of-sale systems to everyday utilities. Explore our case studies for examples of projects.

How much does app or website development cost?

There is no app development cost. The maintenance plan is ₹2,500 per month, billed as ₹7,500 every three months. The yearly plan is ₹25,000, discounted from ₹30,000.

What is included in the maintenance plan?

The maintenance plan covers domain, hosting and maintenance. Share your requirements with us to discuss the details for your project.

How do I get started?

Describe your idea in the input at the top of the page and select a plan. The button opens WhatsApp with your requirements and preferred plan ready for you to review and send.

Can you build a point-of-sale app for my business?

Point-of-sale projects are among the examples in our portfolio, including PoppyBlue POS and Food Inn POS. Tell us about your business and the workflow you want to support.

Do I need technical knowledge to describe my idea?

No. Describe the problem you want to solve, who will use the app or website, and any features that matter to you in your own words.
