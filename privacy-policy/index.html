Privacy Policy

How Imagine handles information when you visit our website or contact us about an app or website.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Information you share

You can describe your project in the form on our home page. When you select “Let’s make it”, the description and your selected plan are placed in a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. We receive that information, along with your WhatsApp number and any other information you choose to share, if you send the message. Please do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information in your initial inquiry.

How we use information

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, discuss requirements, deliver and maintain agreed services, provide support, and handle related billing or legal obligations. If a project requires additional information or processing, we will discuss the relevant requirements with you.

Service providers and third parties

Our site uses hosting services and Google Fonts, which may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests the site or fonts. WhatsApp is a separate service operated by Meta; its own privacy terms apply when you open or send a message there. Hosting providers may retain technical and security logs according to their practices.

Storage and security

We keep information only as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, to provide services, or as required by law. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service or transmission is completely secure. Avoid sending confidential material until an appropriate secure method has been agreed.

Your choices and requests

You can choose not to send the prefilled WhatsApp message. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and any legitimate retention obligations. To contact us about privacy, message +91 98197 08112 on WhatsApp.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version and date will appear on this page.
